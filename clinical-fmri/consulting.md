## Clinical fMRI consulting

Dr. Benjamin offers consulting to hospitals and medical centers looking to develop, improve, or evaluate their clinical language fMRI programs. As a licensed psychologist in Connecticut (CT #8423), he is credentialed to administer and bill for clinical fMRI under CPT codes 70555 and 96020 and brings direct clinical, research, and program-building experience to every engagement.

<img src="/assets/images/language.png" alt="Presurgical language fMRI" class="research-img" style="max-height:240px; object-position: center 30%;">

### Areas of consulting

- **Program design** — selecting cognitive paradigms, MR acquisition parameters, and analysis workflows appropriate to your patient population and institutional context
- **Program establishment** — supporting new programs from initial planning through first clinical cases, including staff roles, reporting, and billing workflows
- **Refinement** — reviewing and improving existing programs: task selection, analysis pipelines, quality assurance, and reporting standards
- **Auditing** — independent review of an existing program against current clinical guidelines and evidence-based standards

### Engagement options

Consulting is available on a one-off basis — a single review, report, or planning session — or as an ongoing arrangement across a program's development. Most work is conducted remotely, but site visits are available where helpful, for example during initial setup, staff training, or hands-on protocol review.
