## Clinical Language fMRI

This site is a collection of resources, software, and information related to clinical language functional MRI for its use in epilepsy surgery planning.

Dr. Christopher Benjamin is a clinical neuropsychologist based in the Epilepsy Clinic at Vancouver General Hospital in British Columbia, Canada. He is a lead on the clinical language fMRI service at Vancouver General Hospital and consults nationally and internationally on clinical fMRI programs. Dr. Benjamin is also an Adjunct Associate Professor in Neurology and Neurosurgery at Yale University, and works clinically as a neuropsychologist in VGH epilepsy and at [Sparrow Neuropsychology](https://www.sparrow.clinic).
