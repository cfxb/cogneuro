## Clinical Language fMRI

This site is a collection of resources, software, and information related to clinical language functional MRI for its use in epilepsy surgery planning.

Dr. Christopher Benjamin is a clinical neuropsychologist based in Vancouver, BC. He oversees the clinical language fMRI service at Vancouver General Hospital and consults internationally on clinical fMRI programs. He also works clinically as a neuropsychologist in the epilepsy program and privately, at [Sparrow Neuropsychology](https://www.sparrow.clinic). Dr. Benjamin is an Adjunct Associate Professor in Neurology and Neurosurgery at Yale University.
